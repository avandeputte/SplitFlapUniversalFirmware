Split-Flap Module Flasher

Puts the module firmware on a split-flap module from this page. There is nothing to install, and nothing leaves your computer.

This browser can't talk to serial ports. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Firefox and Safari don't support it.

Step 1

Connect the flasher

Plug the flasher into USB and wire it to the module's UPDI Interface header:

  • flasher 3V3 to module +3V3
  • flasher GND to module G
  • flasher RXD to module UPDI

The flasher powers the module while it is being flashed. How to build one: FLASHER.md.
